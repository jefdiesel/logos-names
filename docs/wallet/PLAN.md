# Names Wallet: plan for λPrize LP-0021

Names grows into the LEZ wallet the prize asks for, instead of a new app: it already has the
wallet, keys in the names CLI, public and private accounts, payments, the faucet, and per-app
permissions in the module. One install (the Names app + the names module) stays one install.
Spec: lambda-prize `prizes/LP-0021.md` ($20,000, X-Large, first submission meeting every
criterion wins, adoption measured over 2+ months, so it can only finish 2+ months after
testnet 0.4 launches).

## Order

1. Now, in parallel: tokens, testimonial program, provider SDK + approval UI (agents).
2. Merge into `lez-0.4`, then: account management in the app, the two mini-apps on the SDK,
   gas display, e2e CI, docs.
3. Catalog fork (with the registry's modules), release.
4. Launch day of testnet 0.4: deploy the testimonial program, publish, start the adoption clock.

## LP-0022: the Logos Blockchain and many zones

LP-0022 (draft, $10,000) builds on this wallet. Where each criterion stands:

| Criterion | State |
|---|---|
| Blockchain assets: own, send, receive | Done: `names chain balance/send/tx`, the app's Blockchain tab; send to a key or a name's `blockchain` entry. Chain over IPC: `scripts/chain-ipc-e2e.sh` runs the module's `chain_*` through logosctl against the real blockchain_module 0.3.4 on a solo node with no peers (`docs/wallet/e2e-chain-ipc.log`, the 0.15.0 run of 2026-11-06: 34 PASS, 0 FAIL, plus the session stop; the run of 5b37e29 on 2026-11-08 added a bridge deposit section and passed 43, its log was not kept in the repo). It also covers the refusals a verifier found: a hash never sent fails with the node's "no record", a send is still followed after `module unload/load names` (sends kept in `chain-sent.json`), `1,5` is refused rather than read as 15, and a stopped node is named by every method. Upstream: logosctl 0.3.2 turns bare digits into a JSON number, and the host refuses the call before names runs, so logosctl amounts need a comma (`3,000`); `get_transaction` covers only the mempool plus about 10 min, so it is no chain index |
| Staking: stake, unstake, claim | Done as Cryptarchia has it: no stake op (aged notes on the node's keys are the stake), unstake = send off the node's keys, `names chain claim` / Claim. SDP (blend) stake is not wired: the module has no withdrawal call. Chain over IPC: `chain_stake` equals the node's aged notes and vouchers. A claim with nothing to claim is a plain refusal (e2e log). A claim with vouchers was checked on a solo node across the 12:14 UTC epoch boundary on 2026-11-05 (verifier's `v-stake/driver.sh`, log on the Mini at `dev/chain-ipc/v-stake/driver.log`): no Blend peers needed, the claim answered in 0.3 s, and its tx 504090bb was in the block at slot 15600021. **One voucher per claim**: 1656 → 1655 vouchers, 38 claimed, fee 2028 (input 5000, change 2972) from the LeaderFunding key, which must hold funds first. names now reports `claimed`, `fee` and a `warning`. Upstream: the module's `leader_claim()` takes no `max_tx_fee` and has no fee estimate, so a claim can cost 53× its reward and nothing can refuse it in advance |
| Bridging, chain → zone | Done through Basecamp's own node: `chain_split` makes a note of exactly the amount, `chain_bridge` deposits it on LEZ's channel (`channel_deposit_with_notes`), the app's Bridge screen runs both; `names bridge` (CLI, node HTTP API) and Get tokens as before. **On the real testnet:** 2026-11-06 11:01 EST the Mini's Basecamp node split off 10,000,000 (tx e9156c80, fee 2,298) and bridged it (tx ff6b5485) on channel 0101…01 to `Public/9W9huNeAds11pdGpMijrdhTkSQ8upNudRnrZJbNNWfoW`, which held 1 and holds 10,000,001 on 2026-11-09 (`getAccountBalance` on testnet.lez.logos.co). On a solo node: a deposit against a stand-in for the sequencer's `getChannelId`, its fee, landing and the double-bridge refusal (5b37e29). Pending/settled: the tx is followed to `in_block`, then the arrival is the destination's credit (activity); the deposit receipt PDA is not read yet, and the time from block to LEZ credit was not recorded |
| Bridging, zone → chain | Blocked upstream: the LEZ bridge guest panics "Withdraws are disabled" in rc1 and on dev |
| Multi-zone, per-zone sequencer | To do, after the fix/* merge (they rewrite lib.rs, chain.rs, activity.rs). Design: a zone is known by its channel (`getChannelId`), not its URL: a URL whose channel matches the current zone is a sequencer switch that keeps the wallet as it is; another channel is another zone. Each zone has its own LEZ wallet home, since `storage.json` holds `last_synced_block` and private account data that belong to one chain (LEZ `lez/wallet/src/storage/persistent.rs`); the keys are the same on every zone. A zone = `{name, channel, sequencer, wallet, registry}`; `cli_setup()` builds from the current zone, names resolve on the home zone, the bridge uses the current zone's channel. Old configs become zone 0, and the flat `sequencer`/`registry`/`cli_wallet` fields keep being written so a rollback reads them. Activity marks move from per-sequencer to per-zone (`marks_are_per_sequencer`). Test: the official testnet plus a standalone sequencer on the Mini, spun down after |
| Multiple blockchain accounts | Done in the module and the app, not the CLI. `chain_account_new(label)` makes a key in the node's own keystore with the node's `generate_key`, under a title the keystore doesn't hold (`names-N`; the same title again would replace the key and lose its secret), sets the keystore to 0600 and reads the key back; `chain_account_label` keeps labels in the module's `chain-accounts.json` (0600); `chain_account_remove` only hides an empty account made here and never deletes its key. `chain_accounts` rows carry `label`, `title` and `ready`. The app's Blockchain card shows labels and a New account button, and an account the node doesn't use yet can't send and offers no address, Receive or Bridge. The running node uses a new key only after a stop and start in Basecamp. That is an upstream limit, not something names can change: blockchain_module 0.3.4 reads its keystore when the node starts and the running node doesn't see a key added after that (spike on a solo node). What is paid to a key before that, once final, is never seen by the node's wallet (the key is kept, the funds stay unseen), so the note says not to receive to it first. On the lp22-merge build (66b4a8e, lgx fd922dc9…), `scripts/chain-accounts-e2e.sh` on a solo node with no peers: 30 PASS, 0 FAIL (a new key made, 0600, received 5,000 and sent 1,000 back, a hidden account kept its key and showed again when paid 1,200). Not done: a CLI command (the node's HTTP API has no key route in the paths checked), un-hiding, the app inside the real Basecamp (rendered offscreen with a mock node only), and another caller than the Names app over IPC (unit test only) |
| SDK across chain and zones | To do: `propose_transfer` etc. for the blockchain |
| Testimonial + faucet on the blockchain | Testimonial: done in the module, the Provider SDK, the Names app's approval sheet and the testimonial mini-app; no CLI command. `chain_inscribe(from_key, text, send)` (Names app only) inscribes `{"submission":"lp-0022:names-wallet","text"}` on the account's own channel (`sha256("lp-0022:testimonial:" || signer)`), signed by an Ed25519 inscription key per account kept 0600, its fee funded from that account by the node's `wallet_fund_tx`. Any app can ask with `propose_inscription(text)`; the Names app lists the request with `pending_inscriptions` (the asking app, the text, "inscribes on the Logos blockchain", the node's keys with their balances) and approves it with a chosen key through `approve_inscription`. A plan holds nothing: its fee is the node's own, asked with stand-ins of the key's notes as the other chain plans are (asked with the real notes it held them for 31.6 s on a solo node, so looking at the fee stopped the account sending). A send is funded with at most 100,000, and a plan above that is refused. Only a send that failed after its funding keeps that funding (15 min), and a send or split from that key meanwhile says so. A submit the node doesn't answer in time counts as possibly sent: it is followed like a send, the request ends as `failed` ("may not have been sent") and can't be approved again, and the account inscribes nothing else until it lands or 15 minutes pass (unit test; a node that slow was not produced). The text may hold no character that turns it around or shows nothing (the account-label rule). In the Names app an inscription request has its own sheet (the app and its publisher, the words, "public and permanent", the node's accounts with balances and labels, the fee for the account ticked, an extra tick for an unverified publisher); `pending_requests`, the sheet that offers LEZ accounts, no longer lists it. The testimonial mini-app has a "Logos Blockchain" target that asks with `propose_inscription`, needs no connection, and shows the outcome (transaction, account that paid, fee, channel). Both rendered offscreen with a mock bridge (`basecamp/tools/render.py inscribe…`, `apps/testimonial/tools/render.py`), not opened inside the real Basecamp. On lp22-merge with lez-0.4 merged and these fixes (lgx 738a149a…, 2026-11-09), `scripts/lp22-inscribe-e2e.sh` on a solo node with no peers: 32 PASS, 0 FAIL (after three plans the balance and notes are unchanged and another inscription and a send are priced at once; the send is funded at once and pays 1,129 = plan 1,129; the text read back from the block at slot 15989187; a second inscription chained on the tip; `pending_requests` lists 0 inscriptions; an approved request landed, fee 1,109 = estimate 1,109). Not built: a CLI command. Open: logosctl sends bare-number text as a number and the host refuses it (write `str:2026`). Faucet: no Logos Blockchain faucet exists; not started |
| Approval prompts for stake/bridge calls | To do; source verification (LP-0023) is in |
| Gas on blockchain transactions | Estimate before, fee paid after. Every `chain_send`/`chain_split`/`chain_bridge`/`chain_claim` plan carries `fee_about`, the node's own fee for that transaction (its `wallet_fund_tx`, asked with stand-ins of the key's notes and no funding key, because asked with real notes it reserves them: on a solo node a second plan got "available=0" and the send after it was refused); `cannot_pay_fee` when the key's notes can't pay it with its fee (they hold no more than the amount, or the node says "not enough" for every set of the largest), `in_flight` instead when only notes a send from here still spends would pay it (wait for it to land), and the plan is refused when the node refuses the recipient as not a key, as the send is; a claim plan warns when one claim's fee exceeds its reward. Shown in the app's confirm step, the bridge plan and the Stake page (logic.mjs helpers tested; the QML itself not rendered), and in `names chain send` plans (`/wallet/fund`; `fee: about N`, a separate can't-pay line and `cannot_pay_fee` in `--json`). `scripts/chain-fee-e2e.sh` on a solo node: estimate = actual for a send of one input (834) and two (866), a split (834), the CLI plan (834), a deposit (1,614); a claim across the 12:54 epoch boundary of 2026-11-09 (1,290 vouchers of 38): estimate 2,028 = paid 2,028, with the warning. The fees decompose as the Mantle table says at the node's `/mantle/gas-prices`: 834 = 590 + 244 bytes × 1, 866 = 590 + 276, 1,614 = 590 + 590 + 434, a claim 580 + 590 + 429 × storage price (1,599 at price 1 before that boundary, 2,028 at 2 after it), so an estimate is exact at the prices of the moment and can be lower than the fee paid by size × the rise when a price steps between plan and send. No margin is added to an estimate for that: the figure shown is the node's, and what is then paid is bounded only where the call takes a cap (100,000 for a bridge deposit and for an inscription). A claim is paid from the node's leader funding key, which names can't tell from the others, with as many of its notes as it takes (32 bytes × storage price per input): the plan prices a claim with each key's own notes and shows the most (solo node, funding key with 3 × 1,100, across the 21:14Z epoch boundary of 2026-11-09 at gas prices exec 1 / storage 2 before and after: plan 2,092, claim paid 2,092 for two inputs; the earlier single-input stand-in said 2,028 against 2,092 paid). Plans priced with more than two inputs are unit-tested only, not compared on a node; the Names-app-only guard on every chain_* method is read from the code, not exercised against another caller on a node. Fee paid: read from the stored transaction as before. Gas prices in `names chain status`. On the lp22-merge build (66b4a8e, lgx fd922dc9…, CLI c6c0064d…), `scripts/chain-fee-e2e.sh` again gave 23 PASS, 0 FAIL with every estimate equal to the fee taken |
