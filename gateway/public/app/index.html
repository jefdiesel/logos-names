          About and privacy

          Names gives people a name on the Logos Execution Zone (LEZ): post, message and get paid as jef instead of a 32-byte account. This page only reads. To register a name, post or pay, use the Names app in Logos Basecamp on your computer.

          Who sees what

          • This website serves the page and sees that someone opened it. It never sees the name you look up: that stays after the # in the address, which browsers don't send. No cookies, no analytics.
          • The chain's RPC sees your IP address and which record it is asked for. Records are stored under a scrambled ID, not the name, but anyone who guesses a name can find its record.
          • The door. The LEZ testnet's own RPC doesn't let browsers in yet, so this page reaches it through a small relay that adds that permission and only passes ordinary chain calls.
          • Your settings stay on this device.

          Messages

          • End-to-end encrypted. A message is sealed on your phone to the key the other name published (its msg entry) and only their device can open it. Who sent it is inside the sealed part.
          • The messaging node (Logos messaging, Waku) carries messages. It sees your phone's IP address and which inbox a message goes to (a short hash of the recipient's key), not what it says or who sent it. Ours is a temporary relay, set under Settings.
          • Checked on chain. "✓ jef" means the message was sealed with the key jef published on the chain. Anything else shows as "unverified".
          • The messaging key is made on this phone and only opens messages. It can't spend or move money, and it isn't your name's key. Clearing this site's data deletes it.
          • Live only. Messages arrive while the page is open; the network keeps nothing for you yet. Conversations are kept on this device only.
