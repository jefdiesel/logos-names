      Chain (RPC)

      Who sees what

      • This website serves the page and sees that someone opened it. No cookies, no analytics.
      • The chain's RPC sees your IP address and which record it is asked for. Records are stored under a scrambled ID, not the name, but anyone who guesses a name can find its record.
      • The door. The LEZ testnet's own RPC doesn't let browsers in yet, so this page reaches it through a small relay that adds that permission and only passes ordinary chain calls.
      • End-to-end encrypted chat. A message is sealed in your browser to the key the other name published and only their device can open it. The messaging node sees your IP and which inbox a message goes to (a short hash of the recipient's key), not what it says or who sent it.
      • Your keys are made in this browser. The messaging key only opens messages; the wallet key (testnet) can hold tokens. Clearing this site's data deletes them.

      Registering a public name and posting are coming to the browser. Private names, private posts and real money stay in the Names app in Logos Basecamp.
